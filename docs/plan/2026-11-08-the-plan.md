# The plan (8 Nov 2026)

Built from three research files of the same day: `docs/research/2026-11-08-who-pays-and-why.md`, `2026-11-08-memory-for-ai-tools.md` and `2026-11-08-competitors.md`. Read those for every source.

## 1. What Squibby is

**The safety net for AI coding.** Squibby sits in your notch beside Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and Lovable. It checks that "done" is really done, tells you in plain words what your AI changed, fixes what breaks with proof, and undoes any damage in one tap. It works across every AI tool and never touches your git.

Why this and not the other angles the founder raised:
- **Notch status for agents:** at least six apps already do it, mostly free (Vibe Island at $19.99 once, CodeIsland free). It is a feature, not the product.
- **Saving tokens:** a real pain (the founder hit 88% of a weekly limit), but no one, us included, has a measured number yet, and a 2026 study found AGENTS.md style files raised cost by over 20% with no gain. We earn this claim with the lab test in section 4, then lead with it.
- **Memory:** a reason to stay, not a reason to buy. "Never lost" is only claimed once export and restore exist.
- **What is open:** one reviewer, one Undo and one set of notes across every AI tool, for people who cannot read the diff. No vendor will serve its rivals' users.

## 2. Who we sell to first

Solo builders, freelancers and tiny agencies who ship client or revenue projects from a local folder with Claude Code, Codex or Cursor, and never open a pull request.

- Price to test: Pro at $15 a month (founder's choice, 6 Nov), plus a $29 one time "Is this safe to go live" report.
- Second: experienced developers running agents, through the strongest moments (an agent wipes uncommitted work; "done" is not done; tests quietly weakened).
- Proof needed before YC: 20 conversations, 10 installs, 5 people using it 5 days on a real project, 3 payers, 1 measured catch rate.

## 3. The product flow

1. **Install and first minute.** Drop a folder on the notch (or pick it). The first check runs on a practice copy and shows both jobs: a key caught, a bug fixed and proven.
2. **Every day.** You work in your AI tool. When it says done, Squibby reviews at once: Ready, Check these 2 things, or Not safe yet. One tap: Fix it, Copy for my AI, Looks fine.
3. **When it breaks.** Press one key. Squibby finds the line, fixes it, proves it, and keeps Undo ready. If it gets stuck, it says so and offers the next step instead of burning tries.
4. **When it goes wrong.** An agent wipes work or runs something destructive: Squibby took a snapshot the instant before, so it comes back in one tap.
5. **Before going live.** Check before going live, then share the report with your client.
6. **Over time.** Squibby remembers your project and your house rules for every AI tool, and shows how much time and how many tries it saved (once measured).

## 4. Build order (starts after the usage reset, Tue 10 Nov 9:30)

1. **Commit M76** (done, needs one test run with the Mac awake and plugged in).
2. **M77 Safety net.** Snapshot the instant before an agent runs a destructive command (git reset, checkout, clean, rm, database drops), through the agent's own hooks after the person's yes; flag tests or checks the AI weakened, first in the review; warn before a key is pushed.
3. **M78 Memory that is never lost.** Plain files stay the truth; a small search index (SQLite FTS5, local); a start block of about 30 lines with only what the agent cannot see in the code, written once per session so prompt caching works; a "tried and failed" line when the loop breaker stops; export and restore. Learn from claude-mem, Mem0, Cline Memory Bank, Graphiti (all Apache 2.0); Basic Memory and Serena are ideas only (AGPL, GPL).
4. **The lab test and the $10 real run.** Fixtures through the SDK in four conditions (no notes, start block, block plus search, a big dump), repeated, counting tokens including cache and the memory's own cost; plus real fixes and reviews on the practices. Only then: a "time and tries saved" number in the app and on the site.
5. **M79** risky packages (OSV.dev), the weekly summary, payments switched off at $15.
6. **M59 foundations** (click through tests with the approved test tools).
7. **The website, side by side** from step 2 (section 6).

## 5. The feature map

Everything Squibby does stays. Built (✓), built but needs the real Mac (◐), planned (○).

1. **The notch:** ✓ bare at rest, hover panel, grows into Home, live mark, Stop everything; ◐ quick reopen.
2. **Review every change:** ✓ plain words review, readiness line and finding shape, keys and open databases always stop, Fix it / Copy for my AI / Looks fine, Walk me through it; ◐ review the moment your AI is done; ○ weakened tests, key before push, risky packages.
3. **Fix it with proof:** ✓ find the line, fix it, prove it, try ladder, private page check; ◐ sign in handover.
4. **Safety net and Undo:** ✓ snapshots and Undo without git, Last working and save points, what Undo cannot bring back, Undo what it did after Stop, approval cards; ○ snapshot before destructive agent commands.
5. **Faster and cheaper:** ✓ cost pause, spend warning and limit, loop breaker, prompt help, choices when the ask is vague; ○ measured time and tries saved.
6. **Memory:** ✓ project and profile notes with sources, shared AGENTS.md; ○ memory v2 with export and restore.
7. **Learn:** ✓ practices, first run on a practice copy, Explain.
8. **Go live:** ✓ checks and gates, the shareable report, Vercel sign in.
9. **Help on screen:** ◐ Show me where to paste; ✓ Show me how, Point at the problem, hold to talk.
10. **History and Where was I:** ✓ History with Star, Name, Undo; Where was I; ○ weekly summary.
11. **Settings:** ✓ the look (Colour, Character, Layout, Text size) and the behaviour settings; the safety rules stay in code.
12. **Plans and payments:** ○ Free and Pro at $15, the $29 report, switched off until the founder's payment account, lawyer and domain exist.
