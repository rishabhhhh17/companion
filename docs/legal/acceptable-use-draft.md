# Acceptable Use Rules for [Product name] (working name: Squibby)

Last updated: [date]

These rules apply when you use [Product name]'s included usage: the plans where we, not you, pay for the AI model. They are part of the Terms of Use. **[lawyer: say how, and which wins if they disagree.]** If you use your own Claude key instead, Anthropic's own terms and Usage Policy apply to you directly, and these rules do not add anything new for that route. **[lawyer: confirm.]**

## 1. Why these rules exist

When you use included usage, what you type, the pictures of your screen you ask us to look at, and the files you share pass through our server to Anthropic, the company that makes Claude, using our account with them. Anthropic holds us responsible for what goes through our account. So we have to hold everyone who uses it to the same rules. These rules are short on purpose. If you are using [Product name] to build and learn, you will probably never think about them.

## 2. What you may not send or ask for

You may not use included usage to do, ask for, or make anything that Anthropic's Usage Policy forbids. Today that policy has these categories. In plain words:

1. **Breaking the law.** Anything illegal, including selling or making illegal drugs, human trafficking, and copying other people's protected work without the right to.
2. **Harming critical systems.** Attacking or breaking into systems that power things like energy, water, transport, health or the military.
3. **Hacking.** Breaking into computers, networks or accounts you have no right to use. Building viruses, ransomware, spying tools, tools that take over other people's machines, or tools that knock services offline.
4. **Weapons.** Designing or making weapons, including chemical, biological, radiological and nuclear weapons.
5. **Violence and hate.** Encouraging or helping violence, violent extremism, threats, or discrimination.
6. **Invading privacy.** Collecting or misusing other people's private data, or putting together profiles of people without a right to.
7. **Harming children.** Any sexual content involving children, grooming, or any other child abuse.
8. **Cruelty and abuse.** Content that pushes self harm or eating disorders, sexual images of a person shared without their consent, harassment, graphic violence for its own sake, or products built to emotionally harm people.
9. **Lies at scale.** Spreading disinformation, pretending to be someone you are not to mislead people, or medical and scientific misinformation.
10. **Fake campaigns.** Fake accounts and personas, hidden paid promotion, fake news outlets, or tools to run deceptive campaigns.
11. **Attacking elections.** Fake content from candidates or officials, voting misinformation, or keeping people from voting.
12. **Surveillance and censorship.** Tracking people without their consent, finding out who anonymous people are, building tools to watch or censor people, scoring people socially, or using faces and bodies to guess things about them.
13. **Scams and cheating.** Fraud, phishing, spam, counterfeit goods, forged documents, taking advantage of vulnerable people, or passing off others' work as yours where that is not allowed.
14. **Abusing the service.** Making many accounts to get around limits, automated spam, getting around a suspension, getting around regional limits, trying to trick the model into breaking its rules, getting around payment, using outputs to train another AI model, or reselling the service.
15. **Sexually explicit content.** Sexual acts, fetish content or erotic chat.

We can, at any time and without warning if we think we must:
- stop a request;
- pause your account (you cannot use included usage and cannot sign in again until we lift it);
- cancel your plan.

For the most serious ones (for example anything involving children, or a notice from Anthropic that says to stop) we aim to pause the account right away, then look.

**Refunds.** If we pause or cancel your account for breaking these rules, [refund wording: for example no refund for the current period / a refund of unused time only if it was our mistake]. **[lawyer: decide, and check the consumer law of the countries we sell to.]**

**Your own key still works.** Pausing your account stops included usage only. We never touch your own Claude key or account.

## 4. What we can see, and what we cannot

This matters, so we say it plainly:
- **We do not keep a copy of what you send or what comes back.** Our server passes it to Anthropic and keeps only counts (how many tokens, which model, what it cost, when) next to a code that stands for you. It does not keep your prompts, your pictures or the answers.
- Because we keep no copy, **we cannot read back what you sent.** We find out about misuse in two ways: **reports** from people (see section 6), and **notices from Anthropic**, which receives everything that goes through our account.
- Every request we send to Anthropic carries a code that stands for you. It is made from your account number with a secret key that only we hold. It is not your name, your email or anything that identifies you outside our service. If Anthropic reports that code to us, we can work out whose it is and pause that account. They can tell us which account a problem came from without us ever having stored what was said.
- **We may share the code, the dates, and the counts with Anthropic, and with the authorities when the law makes us.** **[lawyer: what exactly may we share, with whom, and under what law. Data protection wording needed: legal basis, retention, where data is processed, rights to access and delete. Decide the country first.]**

## 5. If we pause your account by mistake, or you disagree

- Write to [support address: founder to set up] and include the email you signed up with. Say it is an appeal.
- We will look again within [target: for example 3 working days] and tell you the result. Because we keep no copy of what you sent, we will ask you what happened, and we will rely on what Anthropic told us.
- If we were wrong, we lift the pause and [credit you for the time you lost: lawyer to decide].
- If we were right, we say which rule it was, in plain words, as far as Anthropic's notice lets us. **[lawyer: can we always say why? What if Anthropic asks us not to?]**
- You can use your own Claude key at any time while you wait.

If you see [Product name] being used for something on the list in section 2, or you think an account is being misused, write to [abuse report address: founder to set up]. Say what you saw and when, and how you know it came from [Product name]. Do not send us the harmful material itself. If a child is in danger, or someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first. **[lawyer: the correct reporting route for child safety material in each country we sell to.]**
