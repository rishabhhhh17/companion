## 4. Refund policy: options

Choose one. The recommended default is Option B.

| Option | What it says | Good | Bad |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. No refunds** | All sales are final | Simple | Many countries give a legal right to cancel or refund anyway, so it is often not allowed for consumers. Hurts trust |
| **B. 14 day, no questions (recommended)** | Ask within 14 days of your first purchase and get a full refund of what you paid us. After 14 days, we refund only if the law says we must, or if the app has a serious fault we cannot fix | Fits EU and UK cooling off norms. Easy to explain. Cheap to run. Builds trust for a first-time founder's app | Some people will use it and then refund. The cost is small |
| **C. 30 day money back** | Same as B with 30 days | Stronger trust signal | Longer window for abuse |
| **D. Pro rata for subscriptions** | Cancel any time. Unused full months are refunded on request | Fair | More work, more records |
