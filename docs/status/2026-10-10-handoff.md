## The flow, in 9 moments (what to explain to the founder)
1. Building in Lovable/Cursor/Claude Code, stuck; the AI says "fixed", it is not. The character sits on the screen edge, not watching.
2. Press one key (or hold and speak). A light comes on before anything looks at the screen; typing is never taken over.
3. It reads the project, runs it, and the character flies to the exact failing line, red error or broken button and circles it, with one sentence on what is wrong. "Fix it with me" or "Show me first".
4. It fixes it safely: snapshot first, small change, then checks (build, tests, a hidden browser that presses buttons). Never "done" without passing. Risky things ask first. Stop and Undo always there.
5. Your turn (optional): it points and waits for your click, celebrates when right.
6. Visual receipt: opens the app, circles what changed, "this works now", Undo one click.
7. Why, in two lines, and sometimes one quick check question.
8. Make it live after the safety checks; an "it's live" card to share; the character celebrates; Roll back is one click.
9. Next time it already knows the project (memory files you can edit) and gives three lines on where you left off.
