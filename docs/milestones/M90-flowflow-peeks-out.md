# M90: Flowflow peeks out of the notch

Status: built 14 Nov 2026, tests pass. Not committed. Nothing was spent on a real model: fixtures and stand ins only.

The founder, 14 Nov 2026, after M84 made the hover panel useful but left the notch itself looking the same: the notch at rest changes from bare (F28, 4 Nov) to "Flowflow peeks out". Recorded as F32, which amends F28's update of 4 Nov.

## What it does

- **Where.** A small window beside the notch, as tall as the menu bar row, touching the notch on the left (the usual) or the right. It reaches 10 points under the notch, so its black lip fills the notch's own round corner and the two read as one shape. Only the character's eyes and the top of its head show over the lip's lower edge. On a display without a notch it is beside the small pill; where no menu bar shows it never peeks. It follows the island to its display (Follow you, Built in, Main), and sits beside the wings while they are out.
- **What it shows** (`app/src/peekOut.ts`, first that holds): waiting on you (a question, an approval, Claude Code's waiting hook: the gentle resting face and a small amber dot); proud (a review's checks passed, or a proven fix) for 3.6 s; worried (a check failed, or Not safe yet, or a key caught) for 4 s; watching (any connected tool said busy, or Squibby's own job or review running); hello for 2.4 s at launch; then, after anything went on, resting, sleepy for the last 20 s of the quiet spell, then hidden.
- **When it hides.** Turned off; Home or the notch's hover panel open; a call in front (rule 7); listening, a look at the screen or Show me how (rule 5); Stop everything (until something new happens after it); idle for the chosen time (1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes, 2 by default). The page decides its own rules; the shell (`peekout.rs allowed`) holds the hard ones itself, so a page that has not heard yet can never bring it out.
- **Drawing.** The Draw live renderer (`app/src/flowflow/`, `FlowflowView`, with the reactions hello, watching, proud, worried, sleepy) in the water's own colours, or with Draw live off the existing pictures: glance (watching, waiting), peek (hello, proud, rest), worry and sleep. No new art. It draws Flowflow whatever character is picked elsewhere, as the founder's decision names Flowflow.
- **Motion.** It slides out from behind the notch on `--dur-4` and `--ease-out`, and back on `--dur-exit-sheet` and `--ease-exit`. With Reduce Motion or Less motion, a fade only (the global plain fade rule).
- **Hover and click.** The window lets every click through to the menu bar except on the head (`peekout::on_head`, from each mouse move the ring's monitors already report: the pointer's place only). The pointer on the head is the pointer on the notch: the same hover panel opens (the peek goes in under it), and the shell sends the exit itself once the pointer leaves both. A click opens Home (`island_click`). The panel is borderless and non activating (`make_panel`), never key, at the island's level, and hidden from screen sharing with the rest.
- **Idle cost.** Hidden, the page draws nothing: the live drawing is given back once the slide out ends, and the page sets one timer for its next change by itself, never a loop. The shell looks at the rules twice a second only while the page wants to be out or it is out.
- **A connected tool working** (`runner/src/toolbusy.ts`, new event `hook.busy` with fixtures `m90e1`, `m90e2`): the guard hook of any connected tool sees it run a command, said at most once every 15 s per project, with the project only; the tool's done hook says it is done, as does Stop everything. The pages count it as quiet after 90 s with nothing new.
- **Settings** (General, More options, Notch, first after what pointing opens): `notch.peek` (on), `notch.peekSide` (left), `notch.peekHideAfter` (2 minutes). General's main level already holds ten, the most the settings spec allows.

## Review fixes (14 Nov 2026)

- **A light hides the peek first (rule 5).** `peek_heard` now sets the character's light before the peek hears the event, so the peek reads it on; off the main thread `peekout_heard` waits (at most 300 ms) for the main thread, as `peek_now` does, and for a light or Stop the window is ordered out in that same turn instead of a moment later. Wiring pins in `peekout.rs`.
- **Watching says what it watches.** `hook.busy` comes only from the saved copy signal (the guard hook, after the person's yes) and only for shell commands (`HOOK_MATCHER.guard` is `Bash`). The help, the VoiceOver name and the CHANGELOG now say so: watching shows while a Squibby job or review runs, or while the tool runs commands with that signal set up. A second, non shell busy source (a PostToolUse hook) was not added: it would put a new hook in the person's tool and needs its own yes.
- **The menu bar under it.** The peek sits over the menu bar. With the pointer along the menu bar row near it (the window and 24 points either side, never the notch itself), it slides aside until the pointer leaves that part of the row (`peekout::menu_reach`, the pointer's place only). Coming straight onto the head from below still opens the notch's hover. The setting's help says it sits over the menu bar.
- **The pill.** On a display without a notch the window is as tall as the pill (20 points in a 24 point menu bar) and at its top, so the lip ends where the pill ends: one shape.
- **Proud in the live drawing.** The live water is pinned for the peek (`FlowflowEngine.pinned`): no reaction, pointer or float moves the orb or changes its size, no drops or following bubbles, no sleepy z bubbles (they were cut at the window's top), and proud adds a small smile to its happy arcs so it reads as proud this small.
- **Pictures.** With Draw live off, hello, proud and rest share one picture, and watching and waiting share another (only the dot differs). A test lists the shared ones, and the help and CHANGELOG say the pictures show fewer looks, until the founder supplies art.
- **Pointing.** The VoiceOver name and help say "Point at it as you would the notch", since what pointing does follows the notch's own settings.
- **Two busy projects.** The model keeps each project's last busy time; one project's done ends only its own entry, and the time is the event's own (`ts`), so a busy replayed as a page opens is the past.
- **Old expectations changed on purpose:** the `hook.busy` model test (the shape is now a map per project), the pill frame tests (the pill's height, 20, instead of the menu bar row's, 24), and the DOM test's name match (the new VoiceOver name).

## [verify] on a real Mac

- A crowded menu bar on the 14 and 16 inch MacBook Pros (Xcode or Photoshop on the left, many status items on the right): the peek slides aside as the pointer moves along the row toward it, comes back after, and a click on a menu title beside or under it reaches the menu, never Home.
- A real Claude Code session without the saved copy signal never shows watching; with it, a session that only edits files shows nothing until it runs a command.
- On an external display without a notch, the lip and the pill read as one shape.

Also in `docs/research/technical_research.md` section 20, item 35: no focus taken and no click lost outside the head; the row height and the 10 point reach under the real notch on 14 and 16 inch MacBook Pros; the hover panel opening from the head and closing after; watching's eyes turning toward the screen's middle; hiding at once for a call, a light and Stop from the key and the menu; absent from a screen share; idle cost hidden and out; a real Claude Code guard hook bringing `hook.busy`.
