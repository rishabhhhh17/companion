# M0 desk research: details

Every claim the M0 spikes depend on, checked against live official sources on 6 and 7 Oct 2026, before any spike runs. One research agent per spike checked the claims; a separate agent then re-checked every claim found to differ and tried to prove the first finding wrong. The plain words summary and what changed in the research docs are in `M0-desk-research.md`. This file is the reference.

**Status meanings.** Confirmed: a current primary source agrees. Differs: a current source says something else (the double check verdict follows). Needs the spike: documents cannot settle it; only running it on a real Mac can. Not found: no source either way.

### Where to ask

Use the route Anthropic itself names on the legal page. The 'Authentication and credential use' section ends by telling anyone with questions about permitted authentication methods for their use case to contact sales. The link points to anthropic.com/contact-sales, which redirects to claude.com/contact-sales. That page says the human sales team only takes complex deals, such as 500+ seat deployments and BAAs, and points everyone else to a buying agent chat that it says can escalate to sales. So, in practice: (1) Open the buying agent chat from the contact sales page. Paste the question, point to the legal page's own contact-sales instruction, and ask for escalation to a named person and a written answer. (2) At the same time, open a written ticket from the Claude Console support messenger ('Get help' in platform.claude.com). Support works in writing and emails a copy of each reply, which gives you a paper trail. (3) Optionally, apply to the Claude Partner Network or Claude for Startups to get a named human contact. The Partner Network is free and Anthropic's 12 Mar 2026 announcement says any organization bringing Claude to market can join. The startup program includes office hours with Anthropic's Applied AI team and requires a Console account and a company email. For branding questions only, the SDK overview also says to contact the sales team.

None of the official pages I checked shows an email address for this question, and none explains how to become 'previously approved'. The research docs say 'Email Anthropic'. Change that to 'send a written request through contact sales (buying agent, asking for escalation) and Console support, and keep the replies'. A reply from the buying agent bot or from Fin, the support bot, is not approval. Ask for a named Anthropic employee to confirm in writing. The legal page also says Anthropic may enforce these rules without prior notice, so keep the remote switch even after you get a yes.

### Branding

From the Agent SDK overview: Claude branding is optional. Allowed: 'Claude Agent' (preferred for dropdown menus), 'Claude' inside a menu already labelled Agents, and '{YourAgentName} Powered by Claude' if you already have an agent name. Not allowed: 'Claude Code' or 'Claude Code Agent', and Claude Code-branded ASCII art or visuals that copy Claude Code. The product must keep its own branding and must not look like Claude Code or any Anthropic product. From the legal page (August 2026 onward): you may state accurately, in plain text, that your product has Claude Code preinstalled or runs Claude Code. You may not use the Claude Code or Anthropic names or logos in your product, feature or company name or logo, or in any way that suggests Anthropic built, endorses or partners with you. Any other use falls under the Trademark Guidelines and needs written permission. For Companion: 'Companion, Powered by Claude' and a plain sentence like 'Companion runs Anthropic's Claude Code' are fine. Never use 'Claude Code' in feature names, UI labels, the logo or the store listing title, and avoid any wording that suggests partnership. Branding questions go to the sales team, per the SDK overview.
