# The 30 second demo (draft, for the founder to record by hand)

Written 27 Oct 2026 from the M15 reviews. One clip, one promise: **"Your AI built it. Squibby finishes it, and proves it works."** Short version for captions: **"Fixed. Proven. Undoable."**

Why this one: the market research found that HeyClicky and about ten notch apps already own "a cute helper in the notch", and that nobody we found fixes, proves and undoes across Lovable, Bolt, Cursor and Claude for people who do not code. So the clip leads with the broken app and the proof, and lets Flowflow be the fun.

## What you need
- A real broken export, not the practice: a Lovable or Bolt project that shows a white screen or "Build step failed". The words people use for this: "white screen", "Build step failed", "fix loop", "credits burned", "90% done".
- Squibby with "Hidden from screen sharing" turned off in Settings, so the recording shows it.
- A screen recorder at 1080p or more; the notch in frame.

## The shots (about 30 seconds)

| Time | On screen | Caption |
|---|---|---|
| 0 to 3 s | The broken app in the browser: a white screen. | "My AI built this. It's broken." |
| 3 to 6 s | Pointer to the notch; Home drops down. Click "Find the problem". Flowflow looks. | "One tap." |
| 6 to 12 s | The exact file and line, and two plain sentences on why. The plan with its cost ("about 12 cents"). | "It finds the real line, and says what it will cost." |
| 12 to 20 s | Approve. The notch grows small wings with the working mark. The check chips go green: "Builds", "Tests pass", "Works in a browser". The screenshot of the working page appears. | "It fixes it, then proves it works." |
| 20 to 26 s | The receipt: what changed, the cost, Undo. Press Undo, the page breaks again; press the fix again, green again. | "Not happy? Undo puts it back exactly." |
| 26 to 30 s | Flowflow splashes; the line on screen. | "Your AI built it. Squibby finishes it, and proves it works." |

## Rules for the clip
- Only real runs, never a mock, and never a cost or a time we did not see. If the fix costs 6 cents, the caption says 6 cents.
- No "AI friend" talk and no hype words; it is a tool that finishes things.
- Do not claim it works on any app in the headline (F17); that can be a second clip.
- Never show a real key, a real customer's project, or a private file in frame.

## Where it goes
Build in public posts (X, LinkedIn) and the Product Hunt gallery later. The founder posts it by hand; nothing is posted for them.
