# F18: Home in the notch, the companion comes from anywhere, two key hold to talk (17 Oct 2026)

- **Status:** Founder decision, 17 Oct 2026. Amends F04 (docking) and F17 (launch scope). Not to be reopened without the founder.
- **Source:** the founder, in the build session of 17 Oct 2026: "the docking system and everything about the settings will stay in the notch. The companion can come in from any part of the Mac, wherever you keep it, wherever it has something." "I would prefer a two-click hold to talk." General assistant features: "for now, let's not do it. You can build it out, but I don't want to launch it with that."

## Decision
1. **Home lives in the notch.** Companion's home base (status, the card, Settings, everything you manage) sits at the top center of the screen, around the Mac's notch. It is quiet when nothing is happening and opens downward when you hover or click. On a display without a notch (an external monitor, an older Mac, and Windows later) the same home sits at the top center as a small pill. This **reverses F04's "never the Mac notch"**; F04's reason (it must work on Windows and external monitors) is met by the pill.
2. **The companion comes in from anywhere.** The character is no longer limited to the left and right edges. It lives on whatever edge or corner the person drags it to, per display, and when it has something to show (a problem, a step, a result) it comes to that place, next to the window it is about, never over it, and goes back afterwards. The motion is themed later with the character art (the founder's water idea: it surfaces from the edge). Reduced motion: it appears in place, no flight.
3. **Hold to talk is two keys.** The default is a two key chord, held while speaking and let go to stop. A modifier alone, a double tap or the Fn key are not possible (they need key events, rule 8). The default is Option and Space. If another app already owns it, Companion says so in plain words and the person picks another two key chord in Settings (a short list of chords that are free on this Mac). Control and Space is not offered by default because macOS uses it to switch keyboard languages.
4. **General assistant features are not in the launch.** HeyClicky style agents with memory, app connections, routines, dictation into any app and clicking for you stay out of the friends preview and the launch. They may be built later behind a switch that is off, only when the founder asks.
5. **Part B (rebindable hotkeys, voice settings, accessibility pass, small gaps)** is decided later, when it is needed.

## What stays
Every AGENTS.md rule. In particular: the home and the character are non activating panels and never take keyboard focus by themselves (rule 9); the screen is touched only on a hotkey with the light first (rule 5); nothing reads key events (rule 8); presentation mode and hiding from screen sharing apply to the home and the character (rule 7, M11).

## Settled in the build (17 Oct 2026, item 3)
Details inside item 3, not changes to it.
- Option and Space is registered through the same global shortcut plugin as every other hotkey: the Mac's hot key service reports the keys going down and coming up. Nothing reads key events and nothing asks for Input Monitoring.
- The short list in Settings is Option and Space, Option and Return, and Control and Return. No two key chord is unused on a Mac, so each gives something up while it is hold to talk: Option and Space types a non breaking space on many keyboards, and the two others are macOS's own text keys (Option and Return adds a new line that does not send, in Messages and many other apps; Control and Return adds a line break). Settings says this beside each of the two. Left out on purpose, because losing them would hurt more: Control and Space (keyboard languages), Command and Space (Spotlight), Command and Backquote (the next window of an app), Control and Backquote (the terminal in Cursor and VS Code), Option and Backquote (types accents such as à on a US keyboard, and the key has another label on other keyboards), anything with Tab, Option and Escape (Speak selection), Command and Return (sends in many apps), Shift with anything (pressed by accident while typing), and letters, digits or symbols. (Corrected 17 Oct 2026: the first version of this line said the list leaves out every chord macOS uses, which was not true of the two Return chords.)
- Settings offers only the chords that are free on this Mac: when it opens, it registers each chord not in use and lets it go again at once, and a chord macOS refuses cannot be pressed, with one line saying another app uses it. Pressing a chord tries it again; if another app took it in the meantime, nothing changes and Settings says so in one line.
- That check, and the one at launch, are only as good as macOS refusing a chord another app holds. macOS refuses only when that app asked for the keys alone; Alfred, Raycast and ChatGPT use Option and Space by default and most likely do not, so Companion and that app may share the keys with no notice. Until a real Mac settles it (T13, a launch blocker), Settings, the first run and INSTALL.md name those apps and say what to do if one of them stops opening or talking does nothing.
- The choice is kept on this Mac (`talk-keys.json` in Companion's folder; delete everything removes it). At launch the saved chord is tried, then Option and Space, and nothing else is taken without the person asking. One notice at launch says which keys another app holds and points to Settings, Keys to remember.
- Key repeats while the keys are held restart nothing. A let go that never arrives cannot leave hold to talk stuck, and the microphone still closes after a minute.
- The old Control, Option, Command and V is dropped, not kept as a second binding: nobody has used hold to talk yet (F17), and a hotkey that no hint names would take a key from other apps for nothing.

## Open, to settle on a real Mac
How the island looks and behaves around the notch, in full screen apps, and on a second display; the hands on session checks it.
Hold to talk: whether macOS refuses Option and Space when another app (ChatGPT, Raycast, Alfred, Superwhisper) holds it, or lets both have it (a launch blocker, see T13); whether letting go of Option before Space still ends the hold; and whether the press repeats while held (T13 open items).
