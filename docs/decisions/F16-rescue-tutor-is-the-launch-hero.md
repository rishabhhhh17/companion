# F16. The rescue tutor is the launch hero

- **Status:** Founder decision, 10 Oct 2026. Not reopened.
- **Source:** the founder's feature plan (Companion Feature Plan.xlsx), `docs/research/2026-10-10-market-research.md`, the mentor session
- **Related:** F15, T12, M10 (`docs/milestones/M10-friends-preview.md`)

## Decision
Launch with one job: **"Stuck while building with AI? Press the key. It finds the problem on your screen, fixes it with you, and shows you why."** HeyClicky style "show me on screen", pointed at the person's own project (failing line, terminal error, broken button) instead of generic app buttons, with a tutor loop built into the fix (your turn, then why in two lines). Fun means momentum you can see (reactions, a celebration when proof passes or it goes live, a shareable it's live card), never affection.

## Why
The proven pain is being stuck (fix loops, not understanding code, invisible security holes, the deploy wall), not wanting to learn. Opt in tutoring fails (17% against 98% when built into the work). "AI friend" products failed and companion laws (NY, CA) exclude productivity tools, so this is not marketed as a companion. Google and HeyClicky are broad; we are deep on one project. See the market research report.

## Parked, on purpose
Shared memory across other AI tools, generic app tutorials, dictation everywhere, proactive popups, agents, routines, referral programs. One character first; the founder is supplying the character designs, so no character art is built until they arrive.
