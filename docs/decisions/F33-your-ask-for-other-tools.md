# F33. Your ask, for other tools' runs, comes from the person

Decided by the founder on 27 Nov 2026 ("go with your pick").

## Decision

"Did it do what you asked" (M123) needs the words of the ask. Where they come from:

- **Squibby's own runs:** the request Squibby already has.
- **Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and every other connected tool:** the done card has one optional line, "what did you ask?", where the person types or pastes their ask. Left empty, the check runs as it does today, with no promises.
- **Not done:** reading the ask from the tool's hook input or transcript. The done hook still never reads its input (M75, M85). Asking again later is allowed; changing it needs a new founder decision.

## Why

It keeps the promise that Squibby only hears "done" from other tools, and it still gives every user the feature on a press.

## Consequences

- The typed ask is treated like any request: redacted, clipped, kept only with that task's receipt, never sent anywhere.
- The site says this in chapter 3's long version ("where your ask comes from").
