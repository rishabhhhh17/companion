# F20: Elementals, tools grouped by what they do on your Mac (21 Oct 2026)

- **Status:** Amended by F28 (30 Oct 2026): the Files group and the side-tool switches start off; the defaults line under "Built" below is the old one. Founder decision, 21 Oct 2026 ("go ahead with the Elementals group plan"). Builds on F18 (home in the notch, the companion from anywhere). Names and art are placeholders: the founder supplies the characters, the product name is not final (rule 21: every name lives in the strings file).
- **Idea (the founder's words, condensed):** different tools with different elements, grouped into bigger groups, each group doing a different kind of thing on your Mac. First creature is water based; every new element gives a new power.

## Decision
1. **A group is a set of related tools** (its elements) with one switch, one settings section, one plain line saying what it may do on your Mac, and its own place in the island, Home and the first run. Four groups at launch, made only from what already exists:
   - **Flow** (water, the first creature, named **Flowflow** by the founder on 22 Oct 2026, F24): find the problem, fix it, prove it, undo it, explain it (Rescue, Drive, Proof, Undo, Explain, which task broke it, follow ups).
   - **Guide:** Show me how, walkthroughs and lessons, Your turn, practice, everyday help on screen.
   - **Voice:** hold to talk, spoken answers, Prompt help, the error explainer.
   - **Files:** drop a file on the companion to convert it (F19).
   Group names here are placeholders (strings file only).
2. **Switches follow the permissions.** Turning a group off turns off every tool in it and, where nothing else needs it, stops asking for its permission (Screen Recording, Accessibility, Microphone are asked the first time a tool needs them, rule 8). Stop everything and the safety rules are never part of a group and can never be switched off.
3. **Powers unlock by use, never by streak.** A tool in a group is "new" until the person has tried it once. Home shows, quietly and optionally, which tools of each group they have not tried yet and a one line invitation ("Try Show me how"), never a score, a streak, a level number or a guilt message (rule 16). Nothing is locked behind this: every tool works from the start; unlocking only means the invitation goes away and, later, extra practice for that group becomes visible.
4. **The character reflects the group at work** through named motion and look states (water for Flow: clearer when checks pass, murkier when they fail; other groups get placeholders). The art and the extra groups come from the founder; the code only names the states.
5. **Everything stays customizable** through the settings registry: each group on or off, whether the island shows the group's state, whether Home shows "not tried yet", and a Reset.
6. **Never part of a group:** the safety rules, Stop everything, the daily limit, the key, Delete everything, house rules. F18's general assistant features stay out of the launch; a later group can hold them behind a switch that is off.

## Why (research, 21 Oct 2026, weak evidence, treat as direction not proof)
- Microsoft Research's delight interviews: people enjoy playfulness at work, quickly doing something beyond their own ability, and personalization.
- Personality changes what people enjoy of playful design (so: optional and customizable, not one forced style).
- Practitioners say sporadic rewards after real accomplishment stay delightful longer than constant animation.
- Only about a third of US adults have tried AI companion apps and some of those apps retain users with guilt and FOMO: we do the opposite.

## Built (M12, 20 Oct 2026): choices the build made inside this decision
The catalog is `protocol/groups.json`; `docs/architecture.md` (module `groups`) and T16 ("Added with Elementals groups") describe how it works. Choices the founder did not name, made so every existing hotkey and command belongs to exactly one tool:
- **Where each tool went.** Flow: tasks (Start, Plan, Drive's steps), follow ups, Rescue, Point at the problem (its key and its project check), the checks and the browser check, Undo, Explain and Draw it for me, which task broke it. Guide: Show me how (its key, "Do it for me", the everyday look the stuck key makes in other apps), lessons, Your turn, practice. Voice: hold to talk, spoken answers, Prompt help (read and use a suggestion), the error explainer. Files: dropping a file to convert it.
- **Always on, in no group (the core):** Stop everything, the ask box and its key, Home and the character, projects, the key and updates, Settings, memory and Where was I, Ship (the Online button and going live), Research, the daily limit, Delete everything, house rules. F20 item 6 named some of these; the rest are what F20's four lists left over, so they stay on rather than being switched off by accident. A later group can take Ship or Research behind a switch.
- **A group off never traps work.** Only what ends or takes back something still works with its group off: Undo of earlier work, which task broke it's Undo, a lesson's Stop, dismissing a Prompt help or error result and putting an inserted suggestion back, removing a practice copy, stopping or closing a conversion. Everything else of the group answers with one plain line ("That is switched off in Settings. Turn it back on there to use it.") that names no group. The ask box shows it when a task cannot start, and a press that had set a waiting line closes that line when the command is refused.
- **Switching a group off stops what it is doing, and only that.** Flow ends its tasks, plans, Rescue, checks and the pictures and explanations of a task (the engine finds them by the mode they started in, with the same stop a single Stop uses); Guide the lesson and the Show me pointer and question; Voice the microphone, speech, Prompt help and the error explainer; Files the conversion. Research and Ship have no switch and are never ended by a group going off. What a task already made stays.
- **Tried.** A tool is "tried" after one real use: a finished task, a follow up, a diagnosis, a check that really ran (passed or failed, not cancelled or empty), an undo, a lesson step, a Show me answer outside a lesson, an improved prompt, an explained error, a diagram that was drawn, a which task broke it that reached an answer, a Your turn that was checked; and, noted by the shell, words heard by hold to talk, a line spoken, files converted, a practice finished. A refused try, a cancelled one and a use of a tool whose group is off count for nothing. A task counts only if it finished (the receipt carries how it ended): one the person stopped, one that failed, and one that changed nothing and checked nothing do not. Playing the voice sample in Settings is not a spoken answer. The list holds tool ids only.
- **Defaults.** Since F28 (30 Oct 2026): Flow, Guide and Voice on, with Files, Second Look, Type by voice and Draw it off (and watch routines off). Before F28, all four groups were on; the island shows the working group's name (usual on); Home shows one "not tried yet" line (usual on); the character's group states are names for the art only.
- **Not proven yet.** Built and tested with fakes and by reading the source. Not opened in the built app: the keys let go and taken back on a real Mac, a group switched off in the middle of a task, and the island and Settings lines need a person. The art for the water states is the founder's.
